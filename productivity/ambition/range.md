# Range

Range brings in more fields than the request shows on its surface: their knowledge, tools, and techniques. Running several viewpoints used to cost too much. With agents it is cheap, so the limit is knowing which fields to call.

## Questions

1. Restate the deliverable with verbs in place of nouns: what must it do, for whom, under which limits? A quarterly review is not "a deck". It is "getting busy people to believe a story about cause and effect, and to decide under uncertainty, in 30 minutes".
2. Which fields do those verbs for a living? Assemble the **crew**: the fields that make the thing, the fields that present it, and the fields that attack it.
3. For each field, which working method and which tool would it apply to this material?
4. What would each field check before it signed off, and which check covers the whole result?

## Moves for the rewrite

- **A field earns its place.** Keep a field when you can name its method and a finding or quality it would add that the others would miss. A crew of three to five fields is typical.
- **Methods, in place of voices.** Describe each field by the work it does. To bring in a short seller, ask for what a short seller does to research a position: read the filings, hunt for the number that does not reconcile. The rewrite asks for that work and its findings.
- **Include an adversary.** Name at least one field whose job is to find what is wrong, and give it the raw material, so it can reach its own conclusion.
- **Name the tools.** Where a field works in a tool the plain request would never touch, such as a 3D renderer, a statistics package, or an audio editor, name it and ask for it to be installed and used.
- **One result.** Say who combines the fields' work and what the single deliverable is, so the crew's output arrives as one thing.
